The goal

Replace fragmented operational records with one system tracing every sack from order to delivery to cash.

Solution

Dua Agung Core is the ERP behind a cement distributor’s daily operations. It follows each order from deposit and payment, through the warehouse and loading, to the truck and the driver who delivers it. In parallel, the sales team’s field visits are verified by QR code and GPS, and every transaction flows into a full accounting suite.

As team leader I designed the architecture and built the first phase. I then recruited the team that took it forward; they extended it to more than a hundred modules and kept it in production.

Architecture

CodeIgniter 3 with HMVC modules on MySQL, with a REST API for companion apps, and Telegram and LINE bots for operational notifications.

Highlights

  • 100+ modules across sales, warehouse, fleet, CRM and finance
  • Fleet tracking with driver trip (ritase) and permit approvals
  • CRM visit check-ins verified by QR code and GPS
  • General ledger, fixed-asset depreciation, bank reconciliation, monthly/annual closing
  • Telegram & LINE bot notifications

Outcome

  • Foundation still in production years later — the team I recruited kept extending it to 15,000+ commits and 100+ modules
