Agri-commodity Supply Chain Traceability

Raw-material to packaging traceability with QR/barcode scanning.

POS, ERP & Retail
Client
Agri-commodity company
Role
Lead developer
Period
2021 – 2022
Status
Archived

The goal

Prove the origin of every exported package, from raw material to warehouse to buyer.

Solution

For an agri-commodity exporter, I built a supply-chain system that traces every package back to its raw material. Every packaging run records the crew, the raw material consumed and the products produced, and gives each package a QR-coded number. Scanning the code shows its origin (material, supplier, warehouse, production date and QC status), with sensitive fields visible only to admins and managers.

Sales generate delivery notes. Stock is deducted when goods leave the warehouse, and orders close automatically once every delivery is confirmed.

Architecture

CodeIgniter 3 with HMVC modules on MySQL, with QR codes and mPDF for labels and documents.

Highlights

  • Traceability from raw material to packaged product via QR-coded packaging numbers
  • Packaging runs with crew, material consumed and product output
  • Sales with delivery notes that deduct warehouse stock on dispatch
  • Scan page for customers and inspectors, with the view limited by role

Outcome

  • Delivered in 2022 after a year of iterations with the client
